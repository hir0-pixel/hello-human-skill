## BLOG_HUMAN
Remote work didn't kill offices. It killed the idea that everyone needs the same schedule.

Some teams I talk to are genuinely faster async. Others just have longer Slack threads and fewer whiteboard moments. Both can be true in the same company, different teams.

If you're a manager still debating "remote vs office," you're asking the wrong question. Ask which parts of your workflow actually need a room. We cut our weekly standup from 45 minutes to 15 once we stopped making everyone listen to updates that belonged in a doc.

## ESSAY_HUMAN
Climate policy gets framed like a morality play: either we sacrifice growth or we ignore the science. That binary wastes time.

The harder question is sequencing. Grid upgrades in Texas look nothing like flood adaptation in Bangladesh, and pretending one global checklist fits both is how you get glossy reports nobody implements. I'd start with the infrastructure decisions that lock in emissions for 30 years — power plants, building codes, transit lines — because those are the choices you can't unwind with a press release later.
