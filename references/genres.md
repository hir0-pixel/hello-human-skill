# Genre Guides

## Email

**Human signals:** Direct subject, one ask, uneven paragraph lengths, contractions, first name, no summary paragraph.

**Avoid:** "I hope this email finds you well", "Please don't hesitate to reach out", symmetrical three-paragraph structure, "Additionally" openers.

**Pattern:**
```
Hi [Name],

[One sentence context — why you're writing]

[The ask or info — 2-4 sentences max, include one specific detail]

[Optional: one line of personality or honest caveat]

[Sign-off]
```

## Blog posts

**Human signals:** Strong opening claim (not "In today's world…"), mixed sentence lengths, one anecdote or number, opinion, abrupt or question ending.

**Avoid:** "Let's dive in", section headers that restate the section, tricolon subheads, "In conclusion", meta narration of what the post will cover.

**Structure:** Hook → 2–4 sections of uneven length → end without recap (or one sharp line, not a summary).

## Marketing / landing copy

**Human signals:** Specific outcomes ("cut reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes"), customer language, one clear CTA, confident claims with proof.

**Avoid:** "Revolutionize", "game-changer", "seamless", "robust", feature lists with bold-label bullets, every benefit as equally life-changing.

Pair with [copywriting](../../copywriting/SKILL.md) for conversion structure; Hello Human handles voice.

## Essays (school, op-ed, long-form)

**Human signals:** Thesis in own words (not "This essay will explore…"), evidence with named sources, counterargument you actually engage, variable paragraph length.

**Avoid:** Five-paragraph essay symmetry, "Furthermore" every paragraph, thesaurus verbs, conclusion that only restates intro.

**Note:** Academic integrity policies may require AI disclosure. Human-like ≠ undisclosed authorship where forbidden.

## Text messages / casual DMs

**Human signals:** Fragments OK, lowercase fine, one thought per bubble, no corporate vocabulary, typos only if user's style has them.

**Avoid:** Full sentences every time, perfect punctuation, "Certainly!", helpful-chatbot tone.

## LinkedIn / social posts

**Human signals:** One concrete win or failure, line breaks for rhythm, take a stance, skip hashtag stacks.

**Avoid:** "I'm thrilled to announce", "humbled and honored", inspirational poster endings, emoji bullet lists.

## Cover letters / applications

**Human signals:** One specific reason for this company, one proof point, human reason for interest, irregular paragraph shape.

**Avoid:** Same cadence as 500 other AI cover letters — HR recognizes the template instantly.
